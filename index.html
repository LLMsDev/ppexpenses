Google Workspace Integration

🔒
Data is sent directly to your private Google Apps Script. No third-party servers see your financial information.
  1. Open Google Apps Script Dashboard.
  2. Click New Project.
  3. Copy and paste the template code below into the code editor (replace everything in Code.gs):
  4. Click Deploy (top right) -> New Deployment.
  5. Click the gear icon next to "Select type" and choose Web App.
  6. Configure:
    • Description: Expense Tracker Backend
    • Execute as: Me (your-email)
    • Who has access: Anyone (required for serverless requests)
  7. Click Deploy, authorize the permissions, and copy the **Web App URL** shown. Paste it in the URL field above!
